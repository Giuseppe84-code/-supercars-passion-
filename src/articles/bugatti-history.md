**Bugatti is not just a car brand. It is a philosophy. A belief that engineering is an art form, that speed and beauty cannot be separated, and that if a car is comparable to anything else, it is not good enough. This is the complete story of Bugatti — from a young Italian artist building his first car at age 20 to the most powerful and expensive production cars ever made.**

---

## The Man: Ettore Bugatti

**Ettore Arco Isidoro Bugatti** was born in Milan on September 15, 1881, into one of the most creatively gifted families in Italy. His father Carlo was a celebrated Art Nouveau designer. His brother Rembrandt became a famous sculptor. By age 20, Ettore had designed and built his first car. His guiding philosophy: *"If it's comparable, it's not a Bugatti."*

---

## 1909: The Factory at Molsheim

In **1909**, Ettore founded **Automobiles Ettore Bugatti** in Molsheim, Alsace. The **Type 13** entered competition and finished second at the 1911 French Grand Prix.

---

## 1920s–1930s: The Golden Age of Racing

The **Type 35** (1924) won over **1,000 races**, including five consecutive Targa Florio victories. The **Type 41 Royale** (1926–1933) — only **six examples** built, with a 12.7-litre straight-eight engine — was designed for royalty. Today each surviving Royale is worth tens of millions.

---

## Jean Bugatti and the Type 57 Atlantic

The **Type 57 Atlantic** (1936) by Ettore's son Jean is considered by many the most beautiful car ever made. Four were built from electron alloy with a dramatic dorsal fin. Two surviving examples are worth an estimated **$40 million each** — making them among the most [valuable collector cars](/2026/02/12/supercar-investment-guide/) in the world. A third — the "Lost Atlantic" — disappeared during WWII and has never been found.

---

## Tragedy and Decline: 1939–1987

**Jean Bugatti** was killed testing a car in **1939**, aged 30. **Ettore died in 1947**, aged 65. By 1963, the company was gone. For **24 years**, one of the greatest names in automotive history produced nothing.

---

## 1987: The EB110

Romano Artioli resurrected Bugatti with the **EB110** (1991): quad-turbo **3.5L V12**, **560hp**, AWD, carbon fiber chassis, 212 mph. Michael Schumacher bought one. Only **139** were built before bankruptcy in 1995.

---

## 1998: Volkswagen Saves Bugatti

**Ferdinand Piëch** at Volkswagen had a vision: a car with **1,001 horsepower** and over **400 km/h**. They created the **W16** — two V8s bolted together with four turbochargers — and built a new factory in Molsheim. The same Volkswagen Group that would later save [Lamborghini](/2026/03/01/lamborghini-history/) and fund [Porsche](/2026/03/01/porsche-history/)'s expansion.

---

## 2005: The Bugatti Veyron

The **Veyron 16.4** (2005): 8.0L quad-turbo W16, **1,001 PS**, 0–60 in 2.5 seconds, **253 mph**. Ten radiators. 450 hours of assembly per car. The engineering behind the Veyron was closer to [aerospace technology](/2026/01/14/bugatti-chiron-aerospace-engineering/) than traditional automotive engineering. In 2010, the **Super Sport** hit **267 mph** — a Guinness World Record. **450 Veyrons** were built. VW reportedly lost money on every one.

---

## 2016: The Bugatti Chiron

The **Chiron** (2016): **1,479hp**, **1,600Nm**, 0–60 in 2.4 seconds. In 2019, the **[Chiron Super Sport 300+](/2026/03/03/bugatti-chiron-super-sport-300/)** became the first car to officially exceed **300 mph** — reaching **304.77 mph**, a record that put it at the top of the [all-time top speed rankings](/2026/02/12/fastest-cars-top-speed-record/). **500 units** produced through 2024.

The Chiron era also produced two extraordinary limited editions: the **[Divo](/2026/01/15/bugatti-divo/)** — 40 units focused on cornering rather than top speed — and the Centodieci, paying tribute to the EB110.

---

## 2024: The Bugatti Tourbillon

The **[Tourbillon](/2026/03/01/bugatti-tourbillon/)** (2024): naturally aspirated **8.3L V16** by Cosworth producing **1,000hp** at 9,000rpm, plus three electric motors for **1,800hp total**. Interior with **600+ hand-finished titanium components** by master watchmakers. All **250 units** sold at **$4.1 million each** before the reveal. It competes directly with the [Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut](/2026/03/01/koenigsegg-jesko-absolut/) and [McLaren W1](/2026/03/01/mclaren-w1/) for hypercar supremacy.

## Why Bugatti Is Different

Every hypercar manufacturer claims to build the best. Bugatti is the only one that has consistently proved it — across three completely different eras, under three completely different owners. While [Ferrari](/2026/03/01/ferrari-history/) built its legend on racing and [McLaren](/2026/03/01/mclaren-history/) on engineering precision, Bugatti's identity has always been about transcending what anyone thought was possible.

If it's comparable, it's not a Bugatti. One hundred and fifteen years after a young Italian artist built his factory in Alsace, nothing has changed.
