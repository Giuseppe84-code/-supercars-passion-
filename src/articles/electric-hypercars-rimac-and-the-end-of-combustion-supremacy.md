## Introduction — When Electricity Becomes Dominant

For more than a century, internal combustion defined performance.
More cylinders meant more prestige. More displacement meant more authority. The sound of an engine was the sound of power.
Then something changed.
Electric hypercars — led by Rimac — did not just match combustion performance. They surpassed it.
This is not a transition.
It is a replacement.
This article explores how Rimac redefined performance and why the era of combustion supremacy is coming to an end.

---

## 1. Instant Torque Changes Everything

Electric motors deliver maximum torque instantly.
This creates:

* Unmatched acceleration
* Perfect throttle control
* No gear shifts
* No power lag

Where combustion engines build power over time, electric motors deliver it immediately.
Physics favors immediacy.

---

## 2. Rimac: From Garage Project to Industry Leader

Mate Rimac started as a young engineer modifying an old BMW.
Today, Rimac is:

* A hypercar manufacturer
* A battery and drivetrain supplier
* A technology partner for Porsche, Bugatti, and others

Rimac does not just build cars.
It builds the future infrastructure of performance.

---

## 3. Battery Technology as the New Engine

In electric hypercars, the battery is:

* The fuel tank
* The engine
* The structural element
* The thermal challenge

Rimac’s battery systems:

* Are custom designed
* Use advanced thermal management
* Are integrated into the vehicle structure

Energy storage replaces combustion as the core engineering problem.

---

## 4. Software Becomes Performance

Electric hypercars are not mechanical systems.
They are cyber-physical systems.
Performance is defined by:

* Power distribution algorithms
* Traction control software
* Torque vectoring
* Thermal management code

In electric hypercars, performance is written — not assembled.

---

## 5. Why Combustion Is Losing Its Advantage

Combustion engines are:

* Thermodynamically inefficient
* Mechanically complex
* Difficult to scale beyond current limits
* Environmentally constrained

Electric systems:

* Scale easily
* Improve rapidly with software
* Benefit from global battery research
* Integrate with autonomous and AI systems

The future belongs to systems that evolve.

---

## Conclusion — The New Language of Speed

Speed used to be defined by:

* Cylinders
* Sound
* Displacement

It is now defined by:

* Electrons
* Code
* Control

Rimac represents more than a brand.
It represents a shift in what performance means.
Combustion will survive as nostalgia.
Electric will define supremacy.

### **Rimac Hypercars — Technical Specifications (Concept One, Nevera, Nevera R)**

Rimac hypercars: manufacturer Rimac Automobili; layout for all models mid-engine with four electric motors and all-wheel drive; **Concept One:** production 2013–2016, units approx. 8, four electric motors producing a combined output of approx. 1,224 hp and 1,600 Nm of torque, 0–100 km/h in approx. 2.6 s, top speed 355 km/h, kerb weight approx. 1,850 kg; **Nevera:** production from 2021, units 150, four electric motors producing 1,914 hp and 2,360 Nm of torque, 0–100 km/h in 1.85 s, 0–200 km/h in 4.3 s, top speed 412 km/h, kerb weight approx. 2,150 kg, battery capacity 120 kWh; **Nevera R:** production announced 2024, units approx. 40, uprated version with approx. 2,107 hp, improved aerodynamics and weight reduction, 0–100 km/h in approx. 1.7 s, top speed approx. 420 km/h, kerb weight approx. 2,100 kg; chassis for all models carbon fiber monocoque, battery pack integrated structurally, suspension double wishbone with adaptive dampers, brakes carbon-ceramic discs, advanced torque vectoring and software-controlled dynamics.
