**There are fast cars. There are hypercars. And then there is the Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut — a machine so extreme that its theoretical top speed belongs to the world of jet aircraft, not road cars. Built by a tiny Swedish manufacturer founded in a garage in 1994, the Jesko Absolut may be the [fastest production car ever](https://funaiblog1.wordpress.com/2026/03/03/bugatti-chiron-super-sport-300/) conceived by human beings. This is everything you need to know about it.**

---

## What Is the Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut?

The **Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut** is a limited-production hypercar built by Swedish manufacturer Koenigsegg. Unveiled at the **2019 Geneva Motor Show**, all **125 production slots** were sold before the show ended. Named after **Jesko von Koenigsegg** — the father of founder **Christian von Koenigsegg**. The "Absolut" represents the fastest car Koenigsegg intends to build. Price: **$3.4 million**.

---

## The Engine: 1,600 Horsepower from a 5.0-Litre V8

A **5.0-litre twin-turbocharged V8** producing **1,280 hp** on gasoline and **1,600 hp** on **E85 biofuel**. The crankshaft weighs just **12.5 kg** — the lightest V8 crankshaft in the world. It revs to **8,500 rpm**, going idle to redline in **0.213 seconds**. Each cylinder has its own pressure sensor — a world first. Torque on E85: **1,106 lb-ft**.

---

## The Transmission: Light Speed Technology

The proprietary **Light Speed Transmission (LST)** — a **nine-speed** multi-clutch gearbox that can skip directly from any gear to any other gear in milliseconds. Weighs just **90 kg**. No other manufacturer has built anything comparable.

---

## The Aerodynamics: Built for 330 MPH

Over **3,000 hours** of CFD analysis. Drag coefficient of **0.278 Cd**. No rear wing — replaced by two subtle fins providing just **150 kg** of downforce. Tail section extended by **85 mm**. Frontal area just **1.88 m²**.

---

## The Top Speed: 330 MPH — A Theoretical Limit

Theoretical top speed of **330 mph** (531 km/h). The limiting factor is tire technology — no tire is rated for sustained 330 mph. On June 27, 2024, the Jesko set the **0–400–0 km/h record** in **27.83 seconds**, later improved to **25.21 seconds**. At 249 mph, it was still accelerating hard.

---

## The Lightweight Structure

Weight: **1,390 kg**. Carbon fiber monocoque. Power-to-weight ratio over **1,100 hp per tonne** on E85 — approaching Formula 1 territory in a road-legal car.

---

## The Koenigsegg Story

Founded in a **barn in Ängelholm, Sweden, in 1994** by **Christian von Koenigsegg** at age 22. Nine years to deliver the first customer car (2003). The CC8S, CCR (2005 world record), CCX, Agera, and Agera RS (**277.9 mph** verified in 2017) — each one pushing further. Today: ~400 employees, 25–50 cars per year, all hand-built.

## Why the Jesko Absolut Matters

A company with 400 employees, in a small Swedish city, has built a car whose performance numbers exceed those of manufacturers with 100,000 employees and billion-dollar budgets. The Jesko Absolut is proof that the most extraordinary things in automotive history have always come from people who refused to accept that something was impossible.

Christian von Koenigsegg started in a barn. Thirty years later, he may be building the fastest car the world has ever seen.
