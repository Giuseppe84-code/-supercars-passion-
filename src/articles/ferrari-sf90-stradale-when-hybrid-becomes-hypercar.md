## Introduction — A New Definition of Ferrari Performance

The Ferrari SF90 Stradale is not just Ferrari’s first plug-in hybrid.
It is Ferrari’s declaration that electrification is not the enemy of performance — it is its next evolution.
With a combination of a high-revving V8 engine and three electric motors, the SF90 redefines what speed, control, and efficiency mean in a modern hyper-performance car.
This is the story of how Ferrari entered the hybrid era without losing its soul.

## 1. The Powertrain: A Hybrid System Built for Speed

The SF90 combines:

* A 4.0-liter twin-turbo V8
* Three electric motors
* An advanced high-voltage battery system

The result is:

* Over 1,000 horsepower
* Instant electric torque
* Seamless power delivery
* Electrically assisted turbo response

This is not hybrid for efficiency.
This is hybrid for dominance.

### **Ferrari SF90 Stradale — Technical Specifications**

Ferrari SF90 Stradale: manufacturer Ferrari; production years from 2019; production units not officially disclosed; layout mid-engine, all-wheel drive; engine 4.0-liter twin-turbocharged V8 combined with three electric motors producing a total system output of 1,000 hp and approx. 800 Nm of combined torque, 0–100 km/h in 2.5 s, 0–200 km/h in approx. 6.7 s, top speed 340 km/h; transmission 8-speed dual-clutch automatic; battery 7.9 kWh lithium-ion enabling short electric-only driving; chassis carbon-aluminum composite with extensive use of carbon fiber; kerb weight approx. 1,570 kg; suspension double wishbone front and multi-link rear with adaptive dampers; brakes carbon-ceramic discs with 6-piston front and 4-piston rear calipers; aerodynamics active rear spoiler and underbody airflow management producing significant downforce with adaptive balance.
