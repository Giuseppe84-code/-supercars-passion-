**The Lamborghini Revuelto is not simply a replacement for the legendary [Aventador](/2026/01/15/lamborghini-aventador-svj/) — it is a complete reinvention of what a [Lamborghini](/2026/03/01/lamborghini-history/) V12 flagship can be. With 1,001 horsepower, three electric motors, and 13 driving modes, the Revuelto proves that electrification and raw, visceral supercar excitement are not opposites. They are, in fact, the perfect partners.**

---

## What Is the Lamborghini Revuelto?

Unveiled in 2023, the Lamborghini Revuelto is the direct successor to the Aventador and the first High Performance Electrified Vehicle — or HPEV — in Lamborghini's history. It follows the [Sián](/2026/03/03/lamborghini-sian/), which was the first hybrid Lamborghini but used a supercapacitor rather than a full hybrid system. The Revuelto represents a complete ground-up redesign. Every major component has been developed from scratch.

---

## Engine and Powertrain: The V12 Lives On

At the heart of the Revuelto sits one of the most celebrated engines in automotive history — a naturally aspirated 6.5-litre V12, now in its most powerful and most advanced form ever. Producing 814 horsepower on its own and revving all the way to a spine-tingling 9,500 rpm redline, this engine is a masterpiece of engineering.

Three electric motors complement the V12. Two sit on the front axle, providing all-wheel drive and sophisticated torque vectoring. A third motor is mounted directly within the 8-speed dual-clutch gearbox — a first in Lamborghini history. The combined result is **1,001 horsepower** and an estimated 793 lb-ft of torque.

## A Revolutionary Chassis and Architecture

The Revuelto's [carbon fiber monocoque](/2026/02/12/hypercar-materials-technology/) is 10% lighter than the Aventador's while achieving 25% greater torsional rigidity. The gearbox is now positioned transversally behind the engine — abandoning the unusual front-of-engine layout used by every previous V12 Lamborghini.

---

## Aerodynamics: Active and Aggressive

Lamborghini's [ALA system](/2026/02/12/active-aerodynamics-explained/) — Aerodinamica Lamborghini Attiva — returns in a lighter, more sophisticated form, 80% lighter than conventional hydraulic aero systems. An active rear spoiler works continuously to balance downforce and drag. The [Aventador SVJ](/2026/01/15/lamborghini-aventador-svj/) pioneered this system, and the Revuelto takes it further.

---

## 13 Driving Modes: One Car, Every Experience

The modes range from **Città** — pure electric driving for urban environments — through **Ibrido**, **Trofeo**, and **Corsa**, with each step sharpening the throttle response, stiffening the chassis, and unleashing progressively more of the combined 1,001 horsepower.

---

## The V12 Lamborghini Lineage

The Revuelto carries an extraordinary heritage. The [Miura](/2026/03/01/lamborghini-miura/) (1966) invented the mid-engine supercar. The Countach defined the 1970s and 80s. The Diablo broke 200mph. The Murciélago dominated the 2000s. The [Aventador SVJ](/2026/01/15/lamborghini-aventador-svj/) set [Nürburgring](/2026/02/12/nurburgring-lap-times-explained/) records. And the [Veneno](/2026/03/03/lamborghini-veneno/) and [Sián](/2026/03/03/lamborghini-sian/) proved that limited editions could be art.

The Revuelto is the latest chapter — and possibly the most important, because it proves the V12 can survive the electric era.

---

## Revuelto vs. The Competition

Against the [Ferrari SF90 XX Stradale](/2026/03/01/ferrari-sf90-xx-stradale/) (1,016hp, turbocharged V8 hybrid), the Revuelto trades raw downforce for the emotional purity of a naturally aspirated V12 at 9,500 rpm. Against the [Ferrari F80](/2026/03/01/ferrari-f80/) (1,200hp, $4M), the Revuelto is in a different price league but offers a comparable level of engineering ambition. And in the [eternal Ferrari vs Lamborghini](/2026/02/12/ferrari-vs-lamborghini/) debate, the Revuelto makes a compelling case that Sant'Agata's philosophy — drama, spectacle, and a screaming V12 — is just as valid as Maranello's precision.

---

## Why the Revuelto Matters

By embracing electrification intelligently — using it to add performance rather than replace combustion — Lamborghini has guaranteed the V12's survival until at least 2030 while producing the most capable, most technologically advanced, and most exciting flagship in the brand's 60-year history.

The Raging Bull has never been angrier. Or faster. Or more relevant.
