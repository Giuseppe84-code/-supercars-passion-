**The [Ferrari](/2026/03/01/ferrari-history/) SF90 XX Stradale is not just a car — it is a statement. A declaration that Ferrari has pushed its hybrid hypercar technology beyond anything it has ever produced for the public road. With 1,016 horsepower, track-derived aerodynamics, and only 799 units ever built, the SF90 XX Stradale stands alone at the very top of the hypercar hierarchy.**

---

## What Is the Ferrari SF90 XX Stradale?

The SF90 XX Stradale was unveiled in 2023 as the ultimate evolution of the [SF90 Stradale](/2026/01/15/ferrari-sf90-stradale/), Ferrari's groundbreaking plug-in hybrid flagship. The "XX" designation carries enormous significance in Maranello — it has historically been reserved for Ferrari's most extreme track-only machines, such as the legendary FXX-K Evo and the 599XX. The SF90 XX Stradale marks the first time Ferrari has made an XX-program car fully road-legal, bringing genuine race car DNA directly to public roads.

Built alongside the open-top SF90 XX Spider, the Stradale coupe version represents the purest, most focused expression of Ferrari's hybrid hypercar vision — a machine that bridges the gap between a [Formula 1 car](/2026/01/14/f1-technology-in-hypercars/) and a road-going supercar in a way no Ferrari has ever done before.

---

## Ferrari SF90 XX Stradale: Engine and Performance

At the heart of the SF90 XX Stradale lies a heavily revised version of Ferrari's twin-turbocharged 4.0-litre V8 engine, producing 786 horsepower on its own. Combined with three electric motors — two on the front axle and one positioned between the engine and the 8-speed dual-clutch transmission — the total system output reaches a staggering **1,016 horsepower** and over 590 lb-ft of torque.

This hybrid powertrain sends power to all four wheels, enabling the SF90 XX to sprint from **0 to 60 mph in just 2.3 seconds**. Top speed is electronically limited to 211 mph.

Compared to the standard [SF90 Stradale](/2026/01/15/ferrari-sf90-stradale/), which already produced 986 horsepower, the XX variant extracts an additional 30 horsepower through revised engine mapping, upgraded cooling systems, and a more aggressive hybrid integration strategy.

## Aerodynamics: Formula 1 Technology on Public Roads

Ferrari describes it as the most [aerodynamically](/2026/02/12/active-aerodynamics-explained/) efficient road car it has ever produced. The most dramatic visual change over the standard SF90 is the addition of a **large fixed rear wing**, working in tandem with an active rear spoiler system. At 155 mph, the SF90 XX Stradale generates an astonishing **1,160 pounds of total downforce** — a figure that rivals dedicated racing machinery.

Other enhancements include a redesigned front splitter increasing front downforce by 20%, dual S-Duct channels, underbody vortex generators inspired directly by Formula 1, and a refined rear diffuser. Every surface serves an aerodynamic purpose. Nothing is decorative.

---

## [Carbon Fiber](/2026/02/12/hypercar-materials-technology/) Everywhere: The Lightweight Philosophy

The hood, front and rear bumpers, front splitters, engine cover, under-door panels, rear diffuser, and rear trim are all crafted from lightweight carbon fiber composite. Despite carrying a complex hybrid powertrain with batteries and three electric motors, the power-to-weight ratio is genuinely competitive with dedicated track cars.

---

## Interior: Race Car Focus with Ferrari Elegance

The driver-focused cockpit features Ferrari's signature curved 16-inch digital instrument display and a steering wheel loaded with capacitive touch controls — inspired directly by Ferrari's Formula 1 cars. The driving modes — eDrive, Hybrid, Performance, and Qualify — allow the driver to tailor behavior from silent electric cruising to full-attack race mode.

---

## Price and Exclusivity

Priced at **€770,000** (~$840,000 USD). Ferrari limited production to **799 units**, all sold before official delivery. On the secondary market, values have already climbed to ~$1.6 million — nearly double the original list price. A strong [investment](/2026/02/12/supercar-investment-guide/) by any measure.

## Ferrari SF90 XX Stradale vs. The Competition

In the 1,000+ horsepower hybrid hypercar segment, the SF90 XX has very few genuine rivals. The most logical competitor is the [Lamborghini Revuelto](/2026/03/01/lamborghini-revuelto/) (1,001hp, NA V12 hybrid) — though the SF90 XX's aerodynamic package and track-focused development give it a measurably sharper edge on circuit. Other comparisons include the [Porsche 918 Spyder](/2026/02/12/porsche-918-spyder/) and the [McLaren P1](/2026/02/12/mclaren-p1/), though both are older machines from a previous generation.

Above the SF90 XX sits Ferrari's own [F80](/2026/03/01/ferrari-f80/) (1,200hp, ~$3.5M) — the ultimate flagship. And the previous generation — the [LaFerrari](/2026/01/15/laferrari-hybrid-hypercar/) and [LaFerrari Aperta](/2026/03/03/laferrari-aperta/) — pioneered the hybrid philosophy that the SF90 XX now perfects.

## Why the SF90 XX Stradale Matters

The SF90 XX proves that hybrid electrification does not compromise the soul of a sports car — it amplifies it. Its electric motors exist to make the car faster, sharper, and more explosive than any purely combustion-powered machine could ever be.

For 799 fortunate owners, the SF90 XX Stradale is the most advanced, most exciting, and most raw Ferrari road car ever produced.
