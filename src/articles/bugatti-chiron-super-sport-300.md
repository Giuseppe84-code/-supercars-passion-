*On September 2, 2019, a Modified Chiron Did Something Nobody Had Ever Done Before.*

---

Speed records in the automotive world are common. Manufacturers chase them regularly. Most are forgotten within weeks.

The [Bugatti](/2026/03/01/bugatti-history/) Chiron Super Sport 300+ is different. What happened on a closed stretch of the Ehra-Lessien test track on September 2, 2019 was a genuine milestone — the first time a production-based road car had exceeded 300 miles per hour.

The number was **304.773 mph**. 490.484 km/h.

---

## How You Get to 300 mph

### The Power Problem

The standard [Chiron](/2026/01/14/bugatti-chiron-aerospace-engineering/) produces 1,500 horsepower. For the Super Sport 300+, Bugatti extracted **1,600 horsepower** through revised turbocharger mapping and thermal management improvements. At 300 mph, aerodynamic drag increases with the square of velocity — the engine is not fighting road resistance. It is fighting the air itself.

### The Aerodynamic Problem

The [aerodynamic](/2026/02/12/active-aerodynamics-explained/) package was reworked entirely for minimal drag: extended rear bodywork (longtail), recalibrated rear wing, optimized underbody, minimized body panel gaps. The result was a drag coefficient reduction that made the difference between 280 mph and 300 mph — a gap that sounds small and represents an enormous engineering achievement.

### The Tyre Problem

At 304 mph, each tyre completes approximately 4,100 rotations per minute. Michelin developed a bespoke tyre — the Pilot Sport Cup 2 in a specification never used on any other car. Each tyre was individually X-rayed before the attempt.

---

## The Record Run

Andy Wallace, a Le Mans 24 Hours winner, drove the attempt at Ehra-Lessien's 9-kilometre straight. The run lasted less than a minute. At 304 mph, the car covers 136 metres every second. There is no margin for anything.

304.773 mph. Independently verified. Bugatti had done what nobody had done before.

---

## The Production Car

The production Chiron Super Sport 300+ is limited to **30 examples** at €3.5 million (~$3.9 million). Electronically limited to 440 km/h (273 mph) — still faster than any other production car available to the public.

## The W16 Engine

A W16 is essentially two narrow-angle V8 engines joined at the crankshaft. Bugatti's 8.0L W16 features four turbochargers. At full power, it consumes air at a rate that would empty a normal room in approximately 3 seconds.

---

## What Happened Next — The [Speed Wars](/2026/02/12/fastest-cars-top-speed-record/)

Bugatti's record did not go unchallenged. SSC's Tuatara claim was disputed. [Koenigsegg's Jesko Absolut](/2026/03/01/koenigsegg-jesko-absolut/) simulations suggest 330+ mph capability. Hennessey's Venom F5 has been tested at ~270 mph. The war continues.

But for September 2, 2019 — Bugatti stood alone at the top of everything humanity had ever built on four wheels.

---

## The Chiron Super Sport 300+ in Bugatti's Lineage

The 300+ represents the absolute peak of the W16 era. The [Divo](/2026/01/15/bugatti-divo/) proved Bugatti could build for handling. The 300+ proved Bugatti could build for ultimate speed. Together, they define the full spectrum of the Chiron platform.

Now the [Tourbillon](/2026/03/01/bugatti-tourbillon/) (1,800hp, V16 hybrid) opens a completely new chapter. Its 276 mph top speed is lower than the 300+'s record, but its engineering represents a quantum leap in every other dimension.

On the [collector market](/2026/02/12/supercar-investment-guide/), the 30 Super Sport 300+ units are among the most desirable modern Bugattis, with values expected to climb steadily.
