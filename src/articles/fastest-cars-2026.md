**How fast can a car actually go? In 2026, the answer will surprise you. We are living in the golden age of speed — where electric cars are breaking records once thought impossible, and petrol-powered hypercars are pushing past 300 mph. Here is the complete, simple guide to the fastest cars on the planet right now.**

---

## First Things First: Claimed vs Verified Speed

Before we start, there is one important thing to understand. There are two types of top speed:

**Claimed speed** — what the manufacturer says the car can do. Not always proven in real life.

**Verified speed** — what the car has actually done in a real, independently measured test run.

We will tell you both — because they are very different things!

---

## 🥇 #1 — Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut | 310 mph CLAIMED | ~$3 million

The [Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut](https://funaiblog1.wordpress.com/2026/03/01/koenigsegg-jesko-absolut/) is built by a small Swedish company with one obsession: going as fast as physically possible.

Its twin-turbocharged 5.0-litre V8 produces **1,600 horsepower** on E85 fuel. It weighs just 1,390 kg — lighter than many family hatchbacks. The aerodynamics are designed not to create downforce like most hypercars, but to reduce drag and cut through air like a bullet.

Koenigsegg claims it can reach **310 mph (500 km/h)**. It has never officially run at that speed yet — but the engineering behind the claim is so credible that most experts believe it. It already holds the world record for 0 to 400 km/h and back to zero in just 27.83 seconds.

**In simple terms:** The theoretical fastest car in the world. Sweden vs the laws of physics — and Sweden is winning.

---

## 🥈 #2 — Yangwang U9 Xtreme | 308 mph VERIFIED | ~£200,000+

Here is the big surprise of 2026. The current **verified** fastest production car in the world is not a Bugatti, not a Koenigsegg, not a Ferrari. It is a Chinese electric car made by Yangwang — a brand owned by BYD.

In September 2025, the U9 Xtreme hit **308.4 mph (496 km/h)** on a runway in Germany — officially measured, officially verified. It uses four electric motors producing a combined **3,000 horsepower** from a 1,200-volt electrical system — three times more powerful than a standard family EV.

Only 30 examples will be built.

**In simple terms:** The fastest car ever officially measured. And nobody saw it coming.

---

## 🥉 #3 — Hennessey Venom F5 | 301 mph CLAIMED | ~$2.1 million

Texas-based Hennessey has been chasing the 300 mph record for years — and the Venom F5 finally got there.

A hand-built 6.6-litre twin-turbo V8 produces **1,817 horsepower**. The carbon fiber body weighs almost nothing. The result is one of the most brutally fast cars America has ever produced — raw, rear-wheel drive, and completely unfiltered.

**In simple terms:** An American muscle car taken to the absolute extreme. Loud, powerful, and gloriously mad.

---

## 4️⃣ #4 — SSC Tuatara | 295 mph VERIFIED | ~$1.9 million

The SSC Tuatara has had a complicated history — the company once claimed 331 mph before admitting a GPS error. But in 2022, it officially verified a real-world top speed of **295 mph** — still one of the fastest verified runs in history.

Its twin-turbocharged V8 produces **1,750 horsepower** on ethanol fuel, and its drag coefficient of 0.279 is one of the lowest ever measured on a road car.

**In simple terms:** The underdog that proved itself. One of the most aerodynamically efficient cars ever built.

---

## 5️⃣ #5 — Bugatti Tourbillon | 276 mph | $4.1 million

The new [Bugatti Tourbillon](https://funaiblog1.wordpress.com/2026/03/01/bugatti-history/) may not be the absolute fastest in a straight line — but it is the most extraordinary machine on this entire list. A naturally aspirated **V16 engine** producing 1,000 hp on its own, combined with three electric motors for a total of **1,800 hp**, all wrapped in a chassis built from aerospace-grade carbon fiber with 3D-printed suspension components.

It reaches 62 mph in **2.0 seconds flat** and keeps accelerating all the way to **276 mph**. And it does all of this while featuring a watchmaker-built interior with over 600 titanium components.

**In simple terms:** Not the fastest car in the world — but the most impressive. Speed, luxury, and engineering taken to their absolute limit simultaneously.

---

## 6️⃣ #6 — Rimac Nevera | 258 mph | ~$2.4 million

The Rimac Nevera is the car that proved electric hypercars are serious. Built in Croatia by the same company that now owns Bugatti, the Nevera uses four electric motors producing **1,914 horsepower** and reaches **0 to 60 mph in just 1.85 seconds** — one of the fastest accelerating production cars ever built.

Its top speed of **258 mph** places it firmly in the elite speed rankings — remarkable for a car with no engine at all.

**In simple terms:** The electric hypercar that changed everything. Croatian engineering taking on the world — and winning.

---

## 7️⃣ #7 — McLaren Speedtail | 250 mph | ~$2.25 million

The McLaren Speedtail is something completely different — a **Hyper GT** designed to travel at extreme speeds in total comfort. Its teardrop shape is inspired by the legendary McLaren F1, and its hybrid powertrain produces **1,050 horsepower** driving all three wheels.

At 250 mph, it is not the outright fastest car on this list. But it may be the most beautiful, and almost certainly the most comfortable at extreme speed.

**In simple terms:** The fastest grand tourer in the world. Cross-country in style at 250 mph.

---

## 8️⃣ #8 — Koenigsegg Gemera | 250 mph | ~$1.7 million

The Gemera deserves special mention because it does something no other car on this list can do: it has **four seats**.

A twin-turbocharged V8 hybrid produces **1,700 horsepower** — and the whole family can come along for the ride. 0 to 60 mph in around 2.0 seconds, 250 mph top speed, and enough space for two adults in the back.

**In simple terms:** The world's fastest family car. Absolutely nothing else comes close.

---

## 9️⃣ #9 — Ferrari F80 | 217.5 mph | ~$4 million

The Ferrari F80 does not top this list for outright speed — but its **2.15 second 0 to 62 mph time** makes it the quickest-accelerating car Ferrari has ever built. With 1,200 horsepower from a Le Mans-derived V6 hybrid and over 1,050 kg of aerodynamic downforce at speed, the F80 is devastatingly fast on any road or track.

**In simple terms:** Not the fastest in a straight line — but possibly the fastest everywhere else.

---

## 🔟 #10 — Lamborghini Revuelto | 217 mph | $608,000

The most affordable car on this list — and the one with the most exciting engine. The Revuelto's naturally aspirated **V12 screaming to 9,500 rpm** combined with three electric motors for 1,001 horsepower delivers 217 mph and a 0 to 60 time of 2.5 seconds.

It is not the fastest. But it might be the most exciting to experience.

**In simple terms:** The most emotional car on this list. Close your eyes and listen to that V12.
